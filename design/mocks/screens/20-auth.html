Sign-in and accounts

Guest first, then one tap on Google when saving matters. iPhone adds Sign in with Apple under Google at the same size; Android and web are Google only. Covers linking, merge, sign out, delete, guardian consent for 13 to 17, parent sign-in for under-13s, errors and the web page. Every login surface carries the trust line and only Google's approved label.

Soft login sheet

AUTH-01 rises 1.2 s after the lesson-complete counts finish. Reason lesson_3. It is the only prompt on this lesson complete (R15) and is rationed by the soft-wall budget (R29).

Hard login sheet

AUTH-02 is full height and always closable. After sign-in it closes and the pending action resumes in place.

Google handoff and welcome

Google's own UI runs first. Our sheet then shows a waiting state, and the result screens say plainly what happened.

Account management

Sign out clears this phone for the next person (shared phones). Delete has a 7-day grace period.

Guardian consent (13 to 17)

A teen's guardian says yes with their own Google account, on the same phone or through a link. The guardian side drops the pet and speaks to an adult.

Parents and children

Under-13s never sign in with Google. A parent signs in with their own account and owns the child's account.

Errors and other states

AUTH-30 replaces the buttons inside the sheet that was already open; it is never a new sheet. Cancel in Google's UI is not an error.

Web sign-in

AUTH-29 at app.<domain>/login. The button is rendered by Google (continue_with, pill, outline); One Tap runs here and on the guardian landing only.
