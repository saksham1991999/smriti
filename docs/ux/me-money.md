# Screen specs: Me, Settings, Pro and Notifications

Status: Draft v0.3, 29 Oct 2026. Owner of this file: the me+money agent.

Logic, branches, data and analytics definitions are in [`docs/flows/me-money.md`](../flows/me-money.md) (called "the flow doc" below). Tokens and components come from `DESIGN.md` §2 to §5 and §7. Illustration presets come from `DESIGN.md` §6.2 and are generated by Agnes AI through `skills/visual-director`. Nothing here is drawn by hand.

**How to read the copy tables.** Hindi uses "आप" everywhere, the pet's lines included (ruling R3). "तुम" appears only in little-learner (class 1–5) strings, which are marked "little learner" or "under-10" in their key. Plain spoken Hindi, with loanwords students use (स्ट्रीक, मॉक, लेवल, क्विज़). Hindi strings never assume the student's or the pet's gender: verbs agree with the object, or use the imperative or subjunctive. Named third persons (`{child}`, `{friend}`) take the respectful plural ("चाहते हैं"), which reads as neutral, and an unknown "who" takes the standard default ("कौन पढ़ रहा है?"). `{pet}` is the pet's name (max 12 characters), `{name}` the display name, `{date}` a localised date ("12 Nov" / "12 नवं"), `{n}` a number with tabular figures. Prices are shown exactly as written: ₹1 and ₹99/month (₹99/माह).

**Phase.** Each screen's **Mock priority** line also gives its phase (PRD §17, ruling R8). Screens without a phase note are Phase 1.

**Other agents' screens referred to here:** AUTH-01 (login sheet, soft), AUTH-02 (login sheet, hard), AUTH-08 (merge notice), AUTH-11 (add another sign-in), AUTH-12 (sign out), AUTH-13 to AUTH-16 (delete account), AUTH-18 to AUTH-23 (guardian consent), AUTH-24 (parent sign-in), AUTH-25 (who's learning), AUTH-26 (add a child), AUTH-27 (link to your guardian), AUTH-28 (restore a child), AUTH-33 (save-state chip) in [`auth.md`](auth.md); HOME-01, HOME-02, HOME-06, LRN-05, LES-01, LES-04, LES-08 in [`learn.md`](learn.md); ENT-08, ONB-04, ONB-06, ONB-15 in [`onboarding.md`](onboarding.md); MEM-01, MEM-02, MEM-03, MEM-14, MEM-19, MEM-21, MEM-28, MEM-29, MEM-32, LAB-03, LAB-13, LAB-15 in [`memory-labs.md`](memory-labs.md); MOCK-01, MOCK-19, MOCK-22, PRC-11, PRC-17 in [`practice.md`](practice.md); AI-02, AI-03, AI-05, AI-06, AI-07, AI-09, AI-20, AI-21, SOC-01, SOC-04, SOC-06, SOC-08, SOC-09, SOC-14, SOC-19, SOC-20, SOC-23, SOC-25 in [`tutor-social.md`](tutor-social.md).

## New shared components

These are used below and should be added to `DESIGN.md` §5 and built in `ui-native` and `ui-web` with the same names.

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Meter** | A labelled bar for pet mood and energy. Label and state word above in `label` ("Mood · Happy"), bar 10 px, radius 5, `track` behind. Fill uses `streak` for mood and `gem` for energy, never `error` or `warning`, so a low meter never reads as an alarm. Value is announced as words ("Mood: sleepy, about a third full"), not a number. |
| **DayDots** | A row of 3 to 7 circles for revival quests and streak goals. Done: filled `success` with a check icon. Today: 2 px `primary` ring. To do: `track` outline. Each dot has a weekday label in `caption` underneath. |
| **Banner** | Full-width strip under the top bar, 48 px minimum, radius 16 inset 16 px. Icon + one line + optional action + optional close. Tones: info (`primaryTint`), warning (`warningTint` with `warningText`), offline (`surfaceSunken`). Never `error` fill for billing; billing uses warning tone with a `CreditCard` icon. |
| **Settings row** | 56 px minimum, leading Phosphor icon 24 in `inkMuted`, title in `label`, optional value in `caption` `inkMuted` on the right, then a caret or a switch. Rows group in a `surface` card, radius 20, `line` dividers. |
| **Price block** | The price as text, never inside an image. Big price in `display`, the "then" line in `body`, fine print in `caption` `inkMuted` with at least 4.5:1 contrast. |

## ME · Me tab

### ME-01 · Me
**Purpose:** show the student their pet, plant and progress at a glance and lead to everything personal. **Main action:** open the pet room ("Visit {pet}").
**Entry → exits:** Tab bar (Me), HOME-01 header name → ME-02, ME-04, ME-07, ME-10, ME-11, ME-14, ME-17, ME-20, ME-21, ME-22, MEM-01 (My memory), AI-09 (Your chats), PAY-01, PAY-12, PAY-15, SET-01, SET-13, AUTH-01 (from the AUTH-33 chip or the guest card), AUTH-25 (profile switch, shared phones).
**Layout (top to bottom):**
1. Top bar: title "Me" in `title`; right, a gear icon button (Phosphor `GearSix`, 24, 48 × 48 target) → SET-01; the gem pill (Chip, `gem` icon + balance in tabular figures) → ME-17.
2. Profile header (`surface`, radius `cardLarge` 24, `card` shadow, padding 20): the pet portrait avatar, 88 px circle on `primaryTint`; display name in `title`; under it the AUTH-33 save-state chip ("Only on this phone", "Saving…", "Saved", offline), owned by the auth agent; handle in `caption` `inkMuted` (signed in with a handle only); exam chips (status Chip, exam accent tint); [Edit] ghost button (40) → ME-02.
3. Pet line under the header: avatar 40 + speech bubble, max 2 lines.
4. Stat row: four Stat tiles in one row (two by two below 360 dp width): streak (Phosphor `Plant`, fill, in `streak` gold: the streak is the plant, never a flame, R28), total XP, lessons done, accuracy. Numbers in `title` with tabular figures.
5. Two cards side by side, 12 px apart, radius 20: **Pet card** (`primaryTint`, pet illustration overlapping the top edge, state word, the mood Meter) → ME-04; **Plant card** (`streakTint`, plant illustration, stage name, health chip) → ME-07.
6. Quests card (`surface`): "Today's quests" with three 8 px progress bars and the time left until reset → ME-20.
7. Settings-row group "Learning": My memory → MEM-01 (memory is not a tab, R17), with the due count as a caption ("{n} reviews today"); Your chats → AI-09 (tutor history), hidden for little learners unless the parent turned the tutor on (SET-14).
8. Settings-row group "Collections": Badges ({n}), Card album ({n} of {m}), Streak calendar.
9. Settings-row group "Tools": Downloads ({size} used), PDF books (Pro chip when free).
10. Pro card (radius 20, ages 13 and up only): free: `surface` with a small gold-tag illustration (gold stays on the ₹1 tag only, R11), "Try Pro for ₹1" (or "Get Pro" when the ₹1 offer isn't available) and a secondary button; Pro: `surface` with "Pro · renews {date}" or "Pro · ends {date}" and a caret → PAY-12.
11. Tab bar (82, Me active).

(b) pet card: `pet-cat`/`pet-dog`, 1:1, full body in the current state. (c) plant card: `plant-stage`, 1:1, current stage and health. (d) Pro card: `sunny-object`, 1:1, a blank gold price tag with a ribbon (the "₹1" is overlaid text), decorative.
**States:**
- Loading: skeletons for the header, stats and cards; the pet portrait shows from the local snapshot at once.
- Guest: header shows "Guest" and the guest card replaces the handle and exams; stats work from local data.
- Free vs Pro: the Pro card as above; the PDF books row shows a Pro chip.
- Billing issue: Pro card turns `warningTint` with a `CreditCard` icon → PAY-15.
- Minor 13–17: no change on their own screen.
- Under 13 (managed, including 10–12): no Pro card and no prices anywhere on Me (R14, R15); a "For grown-ups" row → SET-13.
- Little learner: header shows the pet large (120 px) and the name; stat row shows only streak and stickers (card album count); no Pro card, no shop prices, no Downloads or PDF rows; a "For grown-ups" row with a `Lock` icon → SET-13.
- Per-exam app: exam chip is fixed (no edit affordance on it).
- Offline: "Updated {time}" caption under the stats; everything else from the snapshot.
- Dark: cards use dark tints; the pet and plant art is unchanged; shadows replaced by `surfaceRaised`.
**Interaction and motion:** tapping the avatar plays the Rive `wave` trigger (static pose in reduced motion). Pull to refresh: the pet peeks down (UX_FLOW §3). Cards press to 0.97 in `fast` 120 ms with a light haptic. Native stack push to children.
**Accessibility:** focus order follows the layout. The avatar is labelled "{pet}, your cat. Happy." Stat tiles read as one phrase ("12 day streak"). The health chip has an icon and a word, never colour alone. Dynamic type: the side-by-side cards stack to one column above 130% text size.
**Analytics:** `me_viewed`; taps are captured by the target screens' `*_viewed` events.
**Web (≥ 600 px):** two columns: left (360 px) the profile header, stats and Pro card; right a 2-column grid of the pet, plant, quests and collections cards. Tools and collections become a card list. Me sits in the left sidebar.
**Mock priority:** P0 · Phase 1
