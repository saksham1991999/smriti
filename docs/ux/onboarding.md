# Screens · Entry and onboarding (ENT, ONB)

Status: Draft v0.3 (revision 1), 29 Oct 2026. The logic, branches and data for these screens are in [`docs/flows/onboarding.md`](../flows/onboarding.md). Tokens and components are from `DESIGN.md` and `packages/tokens/tokens.json`. Every illustration is generated by Agnes AI through `skills/visual-director` from a named preset (DESIGN.md §6.2); nothing here is drawn by hand.

**Copy conventions (R3).** EN and HI for every string. Hindi uses आप everywhere; तुम only on little-learner screens (class 1–5). Hindi is plain spoken Hindi with the English words students actually say, written in Devanagari: लेसन, क्लास, एग्ज़ाम, लेवल, क्विज़, मॉक टेस्ट, स्ट्रीक, रिमाइंडर, सेटिंग्स, टॉपिक, चैप्टर, प्रोग्रेस, सेव. Verb forms that mark the student's, a pet's or a friend's gender are avoided (passive or verbless phrasing); where that is impossible the string uses ICU `select`. `{curly}` values are filled at runtime. Exam and class names come from the catalogue (`content.exams.name`), never from these tables. A shared glossary in `packages/i18n` should fix the loanword list for every area (flow doc §10).

**Other areas' screens used here:** AUTH-01, AUTH-02, AUTH-04, AUTH-06, AUTH-09, AUTH-18 to AUTH-24, AUTH-26, AUTH-29 ([`auth.md`](auth.md)); HOME-01, HOME-04, HOME-06, HOME-08, LES-01, LES-05, LES-06, LES-08, LES-11, LRN-03 ([`learn.md`](learn.md)); LAB-03, MEM-02, MEM-03, MEM-08 ([`memory-labs.md`](memory-labs.md)); MOCK-02, MOCK-14 ([`practice.md`](practice.md)); SOC-03, SOC-04, SOC-13, SOC-20, SOC-21 ([`tutor-social.md`](tutor-social.md)); ME-01, ME-06, ME-08, ME-10, SET-02, SET-03, SET-04, SET-06, SET-13, SET-14, NTF-02, NTF-03, NTF-06, NTF-07 ([`me-money.md`](me-money.md)).

**Phases (R8).** Each block ends with its phase. Phase 1 unless stated. Little-learner screens and classes 1–8 are Phase 3; social link variants (duel, squad, deck, invite) are Phase 2.

## Shared anatomy of an onboarding step

Every ONB step from ONB-02 to ONB-12 uses one layout, `OnboardingStep`, so the flow feels like one motion:

1. **Top bar**, 56 tall, 20 px side padding: back button (Phosphor `CaretLeft` bold 24, 48 × 48 target, label "Back") on the left; the segmented **Progress** bar (8 px, radius 4, `track` behind, `primary` fill) in the middle, with exactly **n segments**, one per question the path can show (flow doc D14); an optional ghost "Skip" (`label`) on the right. The bar fills one segment per answered question from ONB-02 to ONB-10 and is full on ONB-12. A branch that skips a question removes its segment, so the bar only ever gets shorter. Hidden on ONB-01, ONB-09, ONB-13, ONB-14, and on ONB-08 and ONB-15 (both come after the lesson and are one tap each).
2. **Illustration zone**, about 36% of the screen height, at most 280 px tall on phones, centred. Pets and the plant are Rive views; other art is a still from Agnes.
3. **Headline** in `display` (30/36, 800), `ink`, at most 2 lines. It steps down to `title` (22/28) when the Hindi string or the text size would need a third line.
4. **Helper line** in `body` (16/24, 500), `inkMuted`, at most 2 lines. Optional.
5. **Answer area**: choice cards (the Option card from DESIGN.md §5 used as a choice: `surface`, radius 16, `line` border; selected = `primary` border 2 px **plus** a filled `CheckCircle` badge top-right, never colour alone), chips, or pickers. Scrolls if needed; never hidden under the button.
6. **Pet reaction line** (flow doc D15): one short speech bubble on `surface` with a 32 px pet avatar, under the answers, replaced on each tap. It never states a fact or a number and never blocks the button. Before a pet is picked (ONB-02 to ONB-07) both pets share the bubble.
7. **Pinned primary Button** (56 tall, full width, radius 20, `primary` with its glow), 20 px from the sides and 16 px above the safe area. Disabled until an answer exists (`inkDisabled` on `surfaceSunken`, no glow), except where a default is preselected.

Motion between steps: forward is a shared-axis X slide (`slow` 300 ms, `standard` easing) on Android and the native push on iOS; back reverses it. The progress bar fills with spring `gentle`. The pet bubble swaps with a `base` 200 ms fade and the avatar does `hop`. Reduced motion: a `base` 200 ms fade, no slide, no hop. Taps: Button press scale 0.97 over `fast` 120 ms, light haptic, `tap` sound only when sound is on and the phone isn't silent.

Accessibility defaults for every step: the screen announces "Question {n} of {total}. {headline}" on arrival; focus order is back → headline → helper → answers (in reading order) → pet line → primary button → Skip; every choice card is a single accessible element with role `radio` (single choice) or `checkbox` (multi-select) and its state; the pet line is a polite live region read as "{pet_name} says …" (or "Your buddies say …" before a pet exists); text grows to 200% with grids dropping to one column.

Web (≥ 600 px) defaults for every step: the step renders in a centred card (`surface`, radius 24, card shadow) 560 px wide on `bg`; at ≥ 1024 px the illustration moves to a left panel (440 px) and the question to the right, both inside a 1040 px frame. The primary button sits at the bottom of the card, not pinned to the window. Enter triggers the primary button, arrow keys move between cards, Space toggles a card, Escape closes sheets. Each step is its own URL (`/start/goal`), so browser back works.

### ENT-01 · Splash
**Purpose:** Cover the local boot and hand off to the right first screen within about one second. **Main action:** none (automatic).
**Entry → exits:** app launch → MOCK-14 (an open mock attempt in `mock_local`, checked first), ENT-03, ENT-04, ENT-06, ONB-01, a resumed ONB step, ENT-08 then HOME-01 or HOME-06, HOME-01, HOME-06.
**Layout (top to bottom):**
1. Full-bleed background in the variant's accent (`examAccent.{variant}.light`; dark mode uses `examAccent.{variant}.dark`). All-exams and JEE use `primary`.
2. The white Smriti mark (`brand/logo/svg/smriti-mark-white.svg`, the on-colour version from DESIGN.md §12), 96 × 96, centred, the same as the app icon so the OS splash and the JS splash match. On SSC orange, the spark is white too (DESIGN.md §12).
3. Nothing else: no wordmark, no spinner, no pet (pets wave on ONB-01).
4. Android 12+ uses the system SplashScreen API with the same icon and background; iOS uses the LaunchScreen storyboard with the same layout.

**Copy:**

| Key | EN | HI |
|---|---|---|
| Accessibility label | {app_name} is opening | {app_name} खुल रहा है |

**Illustration slots:** none. The mark is a brand asset from `brand/logo/`, not an illustration.
**States:**
- **Default:** holds for the local boot only (fonts, MMKV, SQLite open and migrations, cached config, session). Never waits on the network (flow doc D11). Analytics, Sentry's non-critical parts and RevenueCat start after the first screen renders.
- **Open mock attempt:** routes to MOCK-14 before anything else; update prompts are held until the attempt is submitted and synced (practice agent's request).
- **Slow device:** if the boot passes 1.5 s, the mark does one gentle scale (1.0 → 1.04 → 1.0, spring `pop`) to show life. No spinner.
- **Dark mode:** dark accent value; the mark stays white.

**Interaction and motion:** exit is a `base` 200 ms cross-fade into the first screen; on first launch, the mark scales down while ONB-01's scene fades in. No haptics, no sound.
**Accessibility:** a single labelled element; screen readers hear the label once. Reduced motion: no scale pulse, plain fade.
**Analytics:** `app_launched` (`cold`, `route`, `ms_to_first_screen`), `config_fetch_failed` (background).
**Web (≥ 600 px):** no splash. The service-worker shell paints `bg` and the mark at 64 px for at most 300 ms while the route loads.
**Mock priority:** P0. **Phase:** 1.

### ENT-02 · Soft update
**Purpose:** Tell the student a newer version is available without blocking anything. **Main action:** [Update].
**Entry → exits:** over HOME-01 after launch (never in onboarding, a lesson, a payment, or while a mock attempt is open) → store page or Play flexible update; [Later] → HOME-01.
**Layout (top to bottom):**
1. Sheet, 40% height, radius 28, grabber, `scrim` behind.
2. Pet line component: the student's pet (40 px avatar, `excited` mood) and a speech bubble on `surface`.
3. Title in `heading`.
4. Primary Button [Update] (56), then ghost Button [Later] (40).
5. Android flexible-update follow-up: once downloaded, a Toast above the tab bar with one action [Restart].

**Copy:**

| Key | EN | HI |
|---|---|---|
| Pet line | I learned new tricks! Update for the best experience. | मुझे नई ट्रिक्स आ गई हैं! अच्छे एक्सपीरियंस के लिए अपडेट करें। |
| Title | A new version is ready | नया वर्ज़न तैयार है |
| Primary | Update | अपडेट करें |
| Secondary | Later | बाद में |
| Toast | Update downloaded. Restart to finish. | अपडेट डाउनलोड हो गया। पूरा करने के लिए ऐप फिर से खोलें। |
| Toast action | Restart | फिर से खोलें |

**Illustration slots:** pet avatar from `pet-cat` / `pet-dog` (1:1, transparent), mood `excited`. Alt intent: "{pet_name} looks excited". Decorative when the pet line is read.
**States:**
- **Default:** as above.
- **Mock attempt open:** held; shown on the next Home after the attempt is submitted.
- **Offline:** not shown (needs the store).
- **Little learner:** not shown to the child; shown in Parent mode (SET-14) only.
- **Dark mode:** sheet `surface` dark, `scrim` dark.

**Interaction and motion:** sheet rises with `slow` 300 ms; swipe down or [Later] dismisses and remembers it for this version and 3 days (flow doc §6.14). Light haptic on tap.
**Accessibility:** focus moves to the title; pet line read first as "{pet_name} says …". Escape or Android back closes like [Later].
**Analytics:** `update_prompt_shown` (`kind = soft`), `update_prompt_action` (`update`, `later`, `restart`).
**Web (≥ 600 px):** replaced by a Toast "New version ready. Reload" with [Reload], shown when a new service worker is waiting.
**Mock priority:** P2. **Phase:** 1.

### ENT-03 · Forced update
**Purpose:** Stop an app that can no longer sync and get it updated. **Main action:** [Update now].
**Entry → exits:** ENT-01, or any screen at a safe point (after a mock attempt is submitted and synced) → store or Play immediate update. No other exit.
**Layout (top to bottom):**
1. Full screen on `bg`, no tab bar, no back.
2. Illustration, 240 × 180.
3. Title in `display`.
4. Body in `body`, `inkMuted`: the reason and the reassurance (2 short lines).
5. Reassurance chip on `streakTint`: Phosphor `Plant` (fill, `streak`) + one line.
6. Pinned primary Button [Update now].

**Copy:**

| Key | EN | HI |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Time for a quick update | एक छोटा-सा अपडेट ज़रूरी है |

| Chip | Your progress is saved on this phone. | आपकी प्रोग्रेस इस फ़ोन में सेव है। |
| Primary | Update now | अभी अपडेट करें |
| Offline line | Connect to the internet to update. | अपडेट के लिए इंटरनेट चालू करें। |
| A11y back note | Update needed to continue | आगे बढ़ने के लिए अपडेट ज़रूरी है |

**Illustration slots:** `pet-cat` / `pet-dog` (1:1, transparent): the student's pet, `curious` mood, holding a small toolbox. Before a pet exists (first launch), `empty-state` (4:3, transparent) with both pets and a toolbox. Alt intent: "Your pet is ready to help with an update".
**States:**
- **Default:** as above.
- **Offline:** button disabled with the offline line under it; retries when the network returns. Downloaded lessons stay usable behind it only if the owner approves flow doc Q9.
- **Mock attempt open:** never shown until the attempt is submitted and synced (flow doc §4 rule 4).
- **Little learner:** same screen, read aloud; the child can't proceed, and the copy is simple enough.
- **Dark mode:** `bg` dark, chip `streakTint` dark with `streakText`.

**Interaction and motion:** fade in `base`. On Android with Play's immediate update available, [Update now] opens Play's full-screen flow; otherwise the store page. Medium haptic on tap.
**Accessibility:** focus starts on the title. Android back does nothing and announces the back note.
**Analytics:** `update_prompt_shown` (`kind = forced`), `update_prompt_action` (`update`).
**Web (≥ 600 px):** a `client-too-old` API error shows the same content in a centred card with [Reload] instead of [Update now].
**Mock priority:** P1. **Phase:** 1.

### ENT-04 · Maintenance
**Purpose:** Explain that the server is down for upkeep and show what still works. **Main action:** [Study offline] when something is usable, otherwise [Try again].
**Entry → exits:** ENT-01, or a server-only action → HOME-01 (offline), the previous screen, or retry.
**Layout (top to bottom):**
Banner mode (default when the app is still useful):
1. A slim banner under the screen's header, `warningTint` with `warningText`, Phosphor `Wrench` icon + one line, 44 tall, dismissible for the session.

Full-screen mode (server-only action, or nothing on the device):
1. Full screen on `bg`, close button top-left (when opened from an action).
2. Illustration 240 × 180.
3. Title in `display`, body in `body` with the end time when known.
4. Pinned primary Button [Study offline] when downloaded content exists, else [Try again]; ghost [Try again] under it in the first case.

**Analytics:** `maintenance_shown` (`mode`).
**Web (≥ 600 px):** banner spans the content column; full screen becomes a centred card.
**Mock priority:** P2. **Phase:** 1.
