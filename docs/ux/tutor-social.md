# Screen specs · AI tutor and social

Status: Draft v0.2 (revision 1), 29 Oct 2026. Owner of this file: tutor + social agent. Logic, branches and data are in `docs/flows/tutor-social.md` (referred to below as "the flow doc"). Tokens and components come from `DESIGN.md` §2–§5 and §7 and `packages/tokens/tokens.json`. No raw hex values. Orchestrator rulings R1–R44 (DECISIONS, with R31 as amended) override anything older.

**Conventions used in this file**
- `{pet_name}` is the student's own pet name. `{name}` is another student as shown to this viewer (R31, amended): an adult's display name; for a minor (under 18) seen by a friend added by invite link or QR, the minor's pet name; for a minor seen anywhere else (leagues, random duels, the All India, school, city and squad boards, the challenge leaderboard, community content, friends added by username search), the minor's `{public_nickname}`, an auto-generated nickname from a fixed word list such as "Sunny Sprout 27" / "धूप अंकुर 27". A minor is always shown with the pet avatar. A minor's real name, typed pet name (outside invite or QR friends) and `@handle` are never shown on public surfaces, because typed text can carry personal data. Minors appear nowhere social before guardian consent.
- Hindi copy uses आप everywhere (R3). तुम appears only in little-learner (class 1–5) strings, which are marked "Little learner". Verbs for the student, the pet and friends avoid gendered forms (imperatives such as करें, passive forms such as "किया जा सकता है", or a noun as the subject). Where that isn't possible, i18n uses an ICU `select` on the subject's grammatical gender, with a neutral fallback.
- Numbers in limits (`{limit}`, `{s}`, `{h}`, `{max}`) come from `/v1/config` flags, never from code. Flag names are listed in the flow doc.
- Illustration slots name a `DESIGN.md` §6.2 preset. Every image is generated by Agnes through `skills/visual-director`. Nothing is drawn by hand. Icons are Phosphor.
- The streak icon is Phosphor `Plant` (fill) in `streak` gold, never a flame (R28). Duo streaks use the same icon.
- Interface strings below are UI copy. Study content (answers, questions, stories, scripts) is generated at runtime or written by the content skills; it appears here only as `{placeholders}`.
- Every new mode (Feynman, voice, role play, duels) has a "?" icon in its header that replays a first-time demo of about 10 seconds, shown once automatically on first use (research X19).
- Little learners (class 1–5) have three tabs: Learn, Labs, Me (R14). They see no Friends tab and, by default, no tutor button. Little-learner variants are Phase 3.
- Every block ends with its mock priority and its phase (R8). Phase 2 covers voice, Feynman, leagues and all social screens. Phase 3 covers parent mode, classes 1–8 and group mocks.
- Event names are from the flow doc §A15 and §B16. Screen IDs from other areas are the ones in `docs/ux/{auth,onboarding,learn,memory-labs,practice,me-money}.md`.

## Part A · AI

### AI-01 · Tutor button (floating)

**Purpose:** Make the tutor one tap away on every study screen, with the current screen as context. **Main action:** Tap to open the tutor.

**Entry → exits:** Shown on the screens listed in the flow doc §A4 (LES-01 lesson player, LRN-03 topic card, LAB-04 sim player, MEM-03 review card back, PRC-05 solution sheet, MOCK-22 solutions review, LRN-11 storybook reader). Never in the PRC-04 drill runner or the MOCK-07/MOCK-08 mock runner (R24) → AI-02 (its little-learner variant when a parent turned the tutor on).

**Layout (top to bottom):**
1. A 56 × 56 circular button, `primary` fill (the same button LES-01 places in the lesson player), `shadow.raised`. Dark mode: dark `primary` value, no shadow.
2. Inside: the student's pet head (40 px, `pet-*` head pose, cropped to a circle).
3. A 20 × 20 badge at the top right of the circle: `surface` fill with a Phosphor `ChatCircleDots` (fill) icon in `primary`. When an answer is streaming: a 2 px progress ring in `primary` around the button. When an unread answer is waiting: an 8 px `primary` dot.
4. Position: bottom right, 16 px from the screen edge, 16 px above the pinned CTA (lesson player) or the tab bar (tab roots). It never overlaps a CTA. It hides while the keyboard is open.
5. First-time tooltip (once per install): a Pet line bubble to the left of the button, at most 2 lines.

**Copy:**

| Key | EN | HI |
|---|---|---|
| Accessibility label | Ask {pet_name}, the tutor | ट्यूटर {pet_name} से पूछें |
| Accessibility hint (with context) | Asks about {context_title} | {context_title} के बारे में सवाल |
| First-time tooltip (pet line) | Stuck? Ask me anything about this. | कुछ समझ नहीं आया? इस बारे में मुझसे कुछ भी पूछें। |
| Unread hint | Your answer is ready | आपका जवाब तैयार है |

**Illustration slots:** `pet-cat` / `pet-dog`, 1:1, head-and-shoulders "curious" pose, transparent, cropped to a circle. Alt intent: decorative (the button has its own label).

**States:**
- Default: pet avatar, badge.
- Streaming (sheet minimised): progress ring animates.
- Unread answer: dot. Cleared when AI-02 opens.
- Hidden: per the flow doc §A4 rules (never in a runner: PRC-04, MOCK-07, MOCK-08), and when the student turned it off in SET-10.
- Offline: shown, with the badge in `inkDisabled`. Tapping opens AI-02 in its offline state.
- Little learner (class 1–5, Phase 3): hidden by default (R14). A parent can turn on "Ask {pet_name} (preset questions only)" in the parent dashboard (SET-14 "Tutor button for {child}"). When on, it shows on HOME-06 and LES-01 only, 64 × 64 for small hands, and opens the little-learner variant of AI-02.
- Dark mode: dark `primary` fill, no shadow.

**Interaction and motion:** Tap: press scale 0.97 over `fast` (120 ms), light haptic, then AI-02 rises (`slow` 300 ms, `standard` easing). On screen entry the button fades in over `base` after the content settles, so it never flashes during transitions. Scrolling down shrinks it to 48 × 48 (pet only, badge hidden) over `base`; scrolling up restores it. Long press (mobile): opens a small menu [Hide on this screen] [Settings]. No sound.

**Accessibility:** Role button, label as above. Last item in the focus order of each screen, before the tab bar. Touch target 56 (48 when shrunk). The badge and ring are not the only cue: the hint text announces "Your answer is ready". Reduced motion: no shrink animation, no ring spin (a static ring at the current progress).

**Analytics:** `tutor_button_tapped` {screen, context_kind}.

**Web (≥ 600 px):** No floating button. An [Ask {pet_name}] secondary Button (40 tall) with the pet avatar sits in the top bar, and the shortcut Ctrl/⌘ + I opens the right-hand tutor panel. The same hide rules apply.

**Mock priority:** P0. **Phase:** 1.

### AI-02 · Tutor chat sheet

**Purpose:** Answer the student's doubt with context from the current card, hints first, with maths, images and follow-ups. **Main action:** Send a question.

**Entry → exits:** AI-01; LES-04 card menu [Ask {pet_name}]; LES-02 answer feedback [Ask {pet_name}]; LAB-04 [Ask tutor] (lab, task and sim state as context); MEM-03 review card back; PRC-05 solution sheet and PRC-07 PYQ [Ask why] (`context_ref = question:{id}`); MOCK-22 solutions review (`context_ref = question:{id}`, only after the mock is submitted); HOME-05 search with no results (the search text pre-fills the input, not sent); AI-15 [Continue in text]; AI-19 [Ask a follow-up] → AI-03, AI-04, AI-05, AI-06, AI-07, AI-08, AI-09, AI-10, AI-12/AI-13 (mic), AI-12/AI-16 (camera), AI-20, LRN-03 topic card (concept chips), PAY-01 paywall, back to the opening screen. Never opened from PRC-04 or MOCK-07/MOCK-08 while a question is live.

**Layout (top to bottom):**
1. Sheet (radius `sheet` 28, grabber, `scrim` behind). Opens at 60% height and can be dragged to full height. From LAB-04 it opens at 40% height with no scrim, so the sim stays visible and keeps running (LAB-04 pauses the sim only when a sheet covers more than half the screen).
2. Header row (56 tall): pet avatar 40 px, title "{pet_name}" in `heading`, under it the free counter in `caption` `inkMuted` (free users only, after the first message). Right side: overflow menu (Phosphor `DotsThreeVertical`, 48 target) and close (Phosphor `X`).
3. Context chip (below the header, 32 tall, `surfaceRaised`, subject `*Text` colour icon of the subject, label "Asking about: {context_title}", trailing `X` to remove). Hidden when there is no context.
4. Message list (scrolls, newest at the bottom):
   - Student bubbles: right-aligned, `primaryTint` fill, `ink` text in `body`, radius 20 with a 6 px corner at the bottom right.
   - Tutor messages: left-aligned, full width minus 16 px, `surface` fill (`surfaceRaised` in dark), pet avatar 24 px above the first message of a turn. Text in `body`. Maths rendered with KaTeX, block maths in a Formula box (subject tint background) that scrolls sideways when wide. Lists and numbered steps allowed.
   - Image slot inside a tutor message: full message width, 4:3 or 1:1 per the generated image, radius 16, tap to open AI-03.
   - "Drawing…" placeholder: same size as the image slot, subject tint background, the pet-with-pencil illustration centred at 96 px, label below, a shimmer bar (8 px, `track` with a `primary` sweep).
   - Under each finished tutor message, an action row (icons 20 px, each 48 targets): thumbs up, thumbs down, "Save as flashcard" (text + icon `Cards`), read aloud (`SpeakerHigh`; free for everyone up to `free.tutor_read_aloud_per_day` answers a day, R35), overflow (`DotsThree`: Copy, Report → SET-24 Report content).
   - The AI label, once per answer, in `caption` `inkMuted`: "Made by AI. It can make mistakes."
   - Follow-up chips: a wrapping row of Chips (32 tall, `surfaceRaised`, `label`), at most 3.
5. Limit, safety and offline cards appear inline at the bottom of the list (AI-06, AI-08).
6. Input row (pinned above the keyboard, `surface`, top `line` divider): camera button (`Camera`, 48), text field (`surfaceSunken`, radius `md` 16, grows to 5 lines, placeholder), mic button (`Microphone`, 48), Send button (48 circle, `primary`, `PaperPlaneRight` in `onPrimary`). Camera and mic show a 16 px lock badge for free users. While streaming, Send becomes a Stop button (`Stop`, `surfaceRaised`).

**States:**
- Empty: greeting, starter chips, input focused (keyboard opens on mobile only if the student came from [Ask why]; otherwise the field is not auto-focused).
- Pre-filled (from HOME-05 with no results): the search text sits in the field with the cursor at the end and the keyboard open. Nothing is sent until the student taps Send, so no message is counted.
- Question context (from PRC-05, PRC-07 or MOCK-22): the context chip reads "Asking about: Question {n}" and the starter chips become "Why is my answer wrong?", "Explain the solution", "Give me a similar one".

- Image pending, slow, failed: as in Copy. A cache or index hit shows the image at once.
- Loading history (reopened session): skeleton bubbles (2 rows) for at most 300 ms, then messages.
- Error: the failed tutor message shows the server-error line with [Try again]. Not counted against the limit.
- Offline: banner "Offline" at the top of the list; Send disabled; draft kept; the offline pet line and [Search downloaded lessons].
- Guest: works the same as a free signed-in user. The counter shows. No sign-in prompt inside the chat, except when they choose Pro on AI-06 or AI-07 (then AUTH-02 with reason `pro`).
- Free: counter; lock badges on camera, mic and "Draw it" (R35). Read aloud works until the daily cap, then the button shows the cap line in a Toast; no paywall.
- Pro: no counter; no lock badges. Near the fair-use cap, no warning until reached (AI-06 Pro variant).
- Minor under 13: off-topic answers become redirects (AI-08 redirect variant). Same layout.
- Little learner variant (Phase 3, only when a parent turned it on in SET-14): no text field, no camera, no mic, no counter, no Pro badges or prices (R14, R15). The input row is replaced by a row of 3 large question chips (56 tall) built from the current story or lesson, plus [More questions] that shows 3 new chips. Every answer has a big read-aloud button, which plays automatically (read aloud is free, R35; little learners have no daily read-aloud cap, because they cannot read long text yet). Answers are short (at most 3 sentences).
- Dark mode: tutor bubbles on `surfaceRaised`, student bubbles on `primaryTint` dark value, Formula boxes use dark subject tints.

Long press on a tutor message: Copy and Report. Pull up at the top of the list loads older messages. No sounds, except the optional soft "ding" when an image lands (follows the sound toggle).

**Accessibility:** Focus order: header (title, counter, menu, close), context chip, messages oldest to newest, chips, input row (camera, field, mic, Send). New tutor text is announced politely when the answer completes (not per token). KaTeX output carries MathML or a spoken-maths label for screen readers. Each image has its alt text and a "Generated image" role description. Lock badges add "Pro feature" to the button label. Dynamic type to 200%: bubbles reflow, the input grows, chips wrap. Hindi uses the Devanagari line-height scale (1.15×). Reduced motion: no slide-in, fades only; no shimmer (a static tinted bar).

**Analytics:** `tutor_opened`, `tutor_message_sent`, `tutor_answer_completed`, `tutor_image_state`, `tutor_followup_chip_shown`, `tutor_followup_chip_tapped`, `tutor_limit_reached` (from AI-06).

On phones under 600 px the sheet layout is used.

**Purpose:** Let the student look closely at a tutor image and keep it. **Main action:** Zoom and read the image.

**Entry → exits:** Tap an image in AI-02, AI-14 board or AI-19 → back to the source; AI-04 (save as flashcard with this image); SET-24 (report image).

### AI-04 · Save as flashcard

**Purpose:** Turn a tutor answer into a review card in one tap, with a path to edit it in the shared card editor (MEM-09). **Main action:** Tap "Save as flashcard" (it quick-saves).

**Entry → exits:** AI-02 action row, AI-03, AI-14 (voice: "save that as a flashcard"), AI-19, AI-20 → quick save to the "From the tutor" deck with a Toast (NTF-05). Toast [Edit] → MEM-09 Card editor, pre-filled ("From your tutor chat"). Toast [View deck] → MEM-08.

**Layout (top to bottom):**
1. No sheet of its own. Tapping the action saves at once, using the `make_flashcard` tool's suggested front and back (front: the question as asked; back: a short answer from the tutor message, plus the image if the message had one).
2. A Toast above the input row: a success check, "Saved to your cards", and the actions [Edit] and [View deck]. It shows for 4 s.
3. If the tutor has no suggestion yet (very short answer, or the tool timed out after 2 s), MEM-09 opens pre-filled with the message text so the student writes the front.
4. In the AI-02 action row the button changes to "Saved" with `CheckCircle` for that message, so the student doesn't save it twice.
